How to play
  1. Everyone gets 3 folded chits. Each word is written on 3 chits.
  2. Turns go round the table. On your turn you hold 4 chits: tap one to pass it to the next player.
  3. Got 3 of the same on your turn? Hit BOOST! to slam your hand on the floor. Quietly.
  4. See a hand on the mat? Slam yours on top, fast! No turns now. Your place in the pile = your points.
  5. The last hand gets a letter and starts the next round. Whoever spells B-O-O-S-T ends the game. Most points wins.
  6. BOOST without a set costs 1 point, and you still have to pass.
